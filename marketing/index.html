          Where the numbers come from

          • Google: rating and review count for every resort and the four Ubud competitors; the newest 20–25 reviews for Ubud, Sanur and Amed.
          • Booking.com: overall and category scores, and the newest 30 reviews for each resort (3 for Ubud, which has only 3).
          • Tripadvisor: overall ratings from its search page.
          • Instagram: followers, and likes and comments on the 9–12 newest posts of each account, as shown to logged-out visitors.
          • TikTok and Facebook: public followers, likes and video views.
          • Topics are found by keywords in what guests liked and disliked on Booking.com in the last 90 days.
