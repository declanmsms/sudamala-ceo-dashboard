          Where the numbers come from

          • Google: rating and review count for every resort and 5–6 comparable resorts in each area (from Google Maps); the newest 20–30 reviews for every resort.
          • Volume-adjusted score = Google stars (out of 10) × our Google reviews ÷ the average number of Google reviews of the competitors in the same area. The ratio stops at 1, so a resort with at least the average number of reviews keeps its full star score. Competitors are scored the same way.
          • Booking.com: overall and category scores, and the newest 30 reviews for each resort (3 for Ubud, which has only 3).
          • Agoda: overall and category scores, and the newest 20 reviews for each resort (fewer where fewer exist), with home country, trip type and whether we replied.
          • tiket.com: overall rating and the newest 6–20 reviews for each resort, mostly from Indonesian guests. The page does not show replies.
          • Traveloka: overall scores for Sanur and Komodo.
          • Tripadvisor: overall ratings from its search page, and the titles of forum threads that mention Sudamala.
          • Instagram: followers, and likes and comments on the 9–12 newest posts of each account, as shown to logged-out visitors.
          • TikTok and Facebook: public followers, likes and video views.
          • Topics are found by keywords in English review text. tiket.com reviews are mostly in Bahasa Indonesia, so they are not counted in topics. Average stars across sites weights each site by its number of reviews.
