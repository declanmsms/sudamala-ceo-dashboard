        How the real version will work

        • Every hour it collects new reviews: Google from the official Google Business Profile API, the booking sites from their public review pages, only where their terms allow.
        • Claude reads each review and adds a sentiment score, topics, the team responsible, urgency, a one-line summary and a suggested reply in the guest's language.
        • A low score (1 or 2 stars) or a hygiene or safety complaint alerts the resort GM within 15 minutes.
        • Nothing is ever posted automatically. A staff member checks and posts every reply.
        • Only public display names are kept; no emails or phone numbers (UU PDP No. 27/2022).

        What the numbers mean

        • Score: every site converted to a score out of 10 (5 stars = 10).
        • Guest mood: average sentiment from −1 (angry) to +1 (delighted), shown as 0 to 100.
        • Waiting for a reply: reviews in the period with no public reply yet.
        • Targets: reply to 90% or more of reviews, within 24 hours; Ubud score 9.0 or higher.
        • Change is against the period of the same length just before.
